> A tactical command deck for tabletop generals, lore-keepers, and armchair strategists who treat every roster like a living campaign.

---

## 🧭 Overview

**Adeptus Army Forge** is a next-generation army management and battle analytics platform built for hobbyists, tournament organizers, and narrative campaign runners who want more than a spreadsheet. It transforms scattered unit notes, scattered PDFs, and half-remembered match outcomes into a coherent, searchable, insight-rich war archive.

Where other tools stop at list-building, Adeptus Army Forge continues into the fog of war — tracking engagements, modeling attrition, and surfacing patterns hidden inside hundreds of skirmishes. Think of it as a war room, a librarian's index, and a statistician's notebook, fused into one responsive, multilingual interface.

This repository hosts the core engine, the analytics modules, the localization layer, and the documentation required to run your own forge.

## 🧩 Why This Exists

Most army managers treat a roster as a static document. Adeptus Army Forge treats it as the opening move of a long conversation between strategy and outcome. The project was born from a simple frustration: after a dozen games, nobody remembers *why* a list won. The data existed. The insight didn't.

This suite closes that gap. It converts the noise of tabletop play into signal, so that the next list you forge is informed by every list that came before it.

## 🏗️ Architecture at a Glance

The platform is organized into layered modules:

1. **Forge Core** — Domain models for factions, units, wargear, and points.
2. **Analytics Engine** — Statistical pipelines that transform raw results into dashboards.
3. **Narrative Layer** — Campaign timelines, lore notes, and event hooks.
4. **Interface Shell** — Responsive, multilingual front-end with theming support.
5. **Interchange Bus** — Import/export adapters for sharing data between groups.
6. **Support Bridge** — Ticketing and live-help integration for 24/7 customer support.

Each layer communicates through well-defined contracts, so you can swap the interface without touching the analytics, or replace the analytics without disturbing the forge.

---

## 📊 Analytics Methodology

Battle analytics in Adeptus Army Forge goes beyond counting wins and losses. The engine models:

- **Attrition Velocity** — How quickly a list loses effective units under pressure.
- **Engagement Tempo** — Whether a force prefers to strike early or grind late.
- **Matchup Asymmetry** — Which opposing archetypes consistently exploit your composition.
- **Synergy Weighting** — Which unit pairings outperform their individual baselines.
- **Momentum Shifts** — Points in a match where outcome probability flips.

These signals are presented in human-readable dashboards rather than raw tables, so a commander can act on them without a statistics degree.
